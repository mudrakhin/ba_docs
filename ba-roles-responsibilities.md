A **Business Analyst (BA)** in a SaaS (Software as a Service) company acts as the bridge between customers, business stakeholders, and product/technical teams. Their main responsibility is to understand business needs, translate them into product requirements, and help deliver valuable software solutions.

## Key Roles and Responsibilities

### 1. Requirements Gathering & Analysis
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Gather customer and business requirements
- Analyze business processes and workflows
- Document functional and non-functional requirements

### 2. Product & Feature Definition
- Create user stories and acceptance criteria
- Define product requirements documents (PRDs)
- Prioritize features based on business value
- Support product roadmap planning

### 3. Stakeholder Management
- Collaborate with customers, Product Managers, Developers, QA, and Support teams
- Facilitate communication between technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Manage expectations and resolve conflicts

### 4. Process Improvement
- Identify gaps and inefficiencies in existing processes
- Recommend automation and optimization opportunities
- Support digital transformation initiatives

### 5. Data Analysis & Reporting
- Analyze customer behavior and product usage metrics
- Generate insights from business and operational data
- Create dashboards and reports for decision-making

### 6. Support Agile Development
- Participate in Sprint Planning, Backlog Grooming, Sprint Reviews, and Retrospectives
- Clarify requirements for development teams
- Validate developed functionality against requirements

### 7. UAT (User Acceptance Testing)
- Define test scenarios and business test cases
- Coordinate UAT with stakeholders
- Ensure delivered solutions meet business needs

# Hard Skills Required

### Business Analysis Skills
- Requirements elicitation techniques
- Process mapping (AS-IS and TO-BE)
- Gap analysis
- Root cause analysis
- User story writing
- Business case development

### SaaS Domain Knowledge
- Understanding SaaS business models
- Subscription and billing processes
- Customer lifecycle management
- Multi-tenant architecture concepts
- SaaS KPIs (MRR, ARR, Churn, CAC, LTV)

### Technical Skills
- SQL for data querying
- API fundamentals (REST, JSON)
- Database concepts
- System integration understanding
- Basic cloud concepts (AWS, Azure, GCP)

### Agile & Project Management
- Scrum and Kanban methodologies
- Jira, Azure DevOps, Trello
- Backlog management
- Sprint planning

### Data & Reporting Tools
- Excel (Advanced)
- Power BI
- Tableau
- Google Analytics
- Mixpanel, Amplitude (common SaaS analytics tools)

### Documentation Tools
- Confluence
- Lucidchart
- Visio
- Miro
- Figma (basic wireframing)

1. Requirement Analysis & User Stories
2. SQL & Data Analysis
3. Agile/Scrum Methodology
4. Stakeholder Management
5. SaaS Metrics (ARR, MRR, Churn)
6. API & Integration Knowledge
7. Communication & Presentation Skills

9. Power BI/Tableau
