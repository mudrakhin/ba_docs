### Business Analyst (BA) Summary

A **Business Analyst (BA)** is a professional who bridges the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. Their primary role is to understand business needs, analyze requirements, and ensure that software solutions deliver value to customers and the organization.

#### Key Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze business requirements.
- Document requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria.
- Communicate with stakeholders, product teams, developers, and QA.
- Support Agile/Scrum activities such as sprint planning and backlog refinement.
- Conduct process analysis and recommend improvements.
- Assist in User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
- Monitor product performance and customer feedback.

#### Essential Hard Skills
- Requirements analysis and documentation
- Process modeling (BPMN, flowcharts)
- Agile/Scrum methodologies
- SQL and data analysis
- API and system integration knowledge
- Jira, Confluence, Miro, Visio
- SaaS business and product knowledge

#### Essential Soft Skills
- Communication and presentation
- Critical thinking and problem solving
- Stakeholder management
- Negotiation and facilitation
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Time management
- Adaptability and learning agility

#### Business Analyst in a SaaS Company
A SaaS BA helps translate customer and business needs into product features, ensuring that the software delivers business value, improves user experience, and supports company growth.

**In one sentence:**  
A Business Analyst ensures that the right solution is built by understanding business needs, defining clear requirements, and coordinating stakeholders throughout the product development lifecycle.
