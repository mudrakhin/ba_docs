For a **probationary Business Analyst (BA)** in a SaaS company, the assessment should focus on three areas: **Business Analysis Competency, Technical Skills, and Professional Behaviors (Soft Skills)**.

# Business Analyst Probation Assessment Checklist

**Assessment Period:** 3-6 Months  
**Rating Scale:**  
- 1 = Needs Significant Improvement
- 2 = Developing
- 3 = Meets Expectations
- 4 = Exceeds Expectations
- 5 = Outstanding

# Key Probation Success Indicators

### Must-Have Indicators
✅ Delivers assigned BA tasks independently  
✅ Produces quality requirements and documentation  
✅ Communicates effectively with stakeholders  
✅ Understands product and business processes  
✅ Works well with Product, Engineering, and QA teams  
✅ Demonstrates ownership and accountability

### Warning Signs
⚠️ Frequent requirement misunderstandings  
⚠️ Poor documentation quality  
⚠️ Missed deadlines without escalation  
⚠️ Difficulty communicating with stakeholders  
⚠️ Low engagement in Agile activities  
⚠️ Limited product/domain understanding

### Decision Guide
- **90-100%:** Confirm employment, high performer
- **75-89%:** Confirm employment
- **60-74%:** Extend probation with development plan
- **Below 60%:** Probation unsuccessful

This checklist can be used by the BA Manager, Product Manager, or Team Lead for monthly and final probation reviews.
